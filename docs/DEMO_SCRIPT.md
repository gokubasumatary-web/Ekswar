# Demo video script (2–3 minutes)

**Goal:** in under 3 minutes, show (1) the problem, (2) your own voice speaking Tamil/Bengali/Marathi, (3) the timing agent at work, (4) honest limits.

**Before recording**
- Start the page: `.venv\Scripts\streamlit run app.py`. Use the 2-minute Diwali video and your voice sample (both cached, so the run takes seconds and costs nothing).
- Close other tabs, browser zoom 100 %, screen recorder at 1080p (Windows: `Win + G` or OBS).
- Do **not** show `.env`, the terminal with keys, or the Gnani/Google dashboards.

| Time | Screen | What to say (roughly) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | You on camera, or the hero section with the greeting cycling | "I make videos in Hindi. But most of India doesn't speak Hindi. Ekswar dubs my video into Tamil, Bengali and Marathi in **my own voice**." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Scroll slowly: progress bar, the 4 steps, the "Why Ekswar" words lighting up | "It listens with Gnani Prisma, translates with Gemini, speaks with Gnani voice cloning, and then a **timing agent** fits every line to my original timing." |
| 0:30–0:50 | Upload video + voice, pick Tamil + Bengali + Marathi, type your name, **tick consent**, click Dub | "I upload my video and a 20-second voice sample. Consent is required: it must be my voice." |
| 0:50–1:05 | Progress steps 1→9 | "Separating my voice from the music, transcribing, splitting at my pauses, translating, speaking, timing, checking quality, mixing." |
| 1:05–1:40 | **Tamil tab:** play original, then the dub (10 s each) | "Same video, same music, but I'm speaking Tamil. I don't speak Tamil." |
| 1:40–2:05 | Scroll to **"What the timing agent did"** + table; point at *Sped up*, *Rewritten*, *Slowed* rows | "Translated lines don't fit the original time. Here the agent sped this line up, **rewrote this one shorter**, and slowed these slightly so the voice starts and ends with me." |
| 2:05–2:20 | Bengali or Marathi tab, play 5–8 s | "Bengali… Marathi. Same voice." |
| 2:20–2:40 | Point at the QA column and the AI-dubbed label | "Gnani listens to every dubbed line to check it says what we meant. Everything is labelled AI-dubbed. It doesn't do lip-sync: it matches timing, not lip movement." |
| 2:40–2:55 | Hero / GitHub page | "Built on Gnani Prisma and Gnani voice cloning. Ekswar: one voice, every Indian language." |

**Tips**
- Rehearse once; keep each clip short; speak slowly.
- If the run is slow on recording day (Gemini or Gnani busy), record with the cached run. Say nothing untrue about speed.
- Show the **rewritten** row: it's the clearest proof of the timing agent. In our demo it's in Tamil.
